Student Terms & Rules

Please read the institutional guidelines carefully. Compliance with these rules is necessary for all academic terms.

General Rules

Every student must carry their official institutional ID card at all times while on campus. Failure to produce the ID card upon request may result in denial of access to facilities.

Attendance

Students must maintain a minimum of 75% aggregate attendance across all registered courses to qualify for final evaluations.
Absences due to medical reasons must be backed by a certified doctor's report submitted within three days.

Examination

Any form of academic malpractice, cheating, or copying during exams is strictly prohibited. Involved candidates will face immediate suspension from the current testing module.

Candidates must report to the examination center at least 30 minutes before the paper starts. Late entries beyond 15 minutes will face a complete entry ban for that exam session.

Assignment Submission

All laboratory and project code assignments must be uploaded directly to your GitHub repository. The final link must be submitted to the instructor before the strict weekly deadline of Sunday midnight.

Explanatory Note: Submissions sent via personal emails or local chat applications will not be recognized or evaluated by the portal.

Discipline

Maintaining strict decorum and absolute silence inside the research laboratory is mandatory.
Eating, drinking, or operating personal electronic music systems inside the facility area is explicitly restricted.
