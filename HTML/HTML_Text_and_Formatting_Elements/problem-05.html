Biology


Biology is the scientific study of life. It is a natural science with a broad scope but has several unifying themes that tie it together as a single, coherent field.

Cell Definition: The cell is the basic structural, functional, and biological unit of all known living organisms. Cells are often called the "building blocks of life".

Energy Currency: Inside cells, energy is stored and transferred using a special molecule known as ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate).
