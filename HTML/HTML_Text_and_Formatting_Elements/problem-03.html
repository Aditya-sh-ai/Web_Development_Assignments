THE TIMES OF ADITYA

More than 20 injured as moderate earthquake shakes northeast Japan

The Japan Meteorological Agency recorded the earthquake at a preliminary magnitude of 5.9, with its epicentre in southern Ibaraki prefecture at a depth of 70 kilometres. The US Geological Survey measured the quake at magnitude 5.8.


Injuries reported across four prefectures Officials said nine people were injured in Saitama, another nine in Kanagawa, four in Chiba and one in Ibaraki.


Many of those injured fell or struck furniture and doors after the earthquake caught them by surprise while they were asleep. They rushed out of bed in panic as the tremor struck.


Among the injured was a woman in her 80s who fell from her bed and suffered a serious hip injury in Yokohama, Kanagawa prefecture, the Yomiuri newspaper


A 23-year-old man told the Asahi newspaper that an earthquake alert sounded seconds before he felt a vertical jolt while shopping at a discount store in Tokyo's eastern Taito district. As the building shook, he feared merchandise could fall from the high shelves.


Train services disrupted The earthquake caused delays to express trains operating between Tokyo and Narita Airport, along with other local services serving northeastern areas.
