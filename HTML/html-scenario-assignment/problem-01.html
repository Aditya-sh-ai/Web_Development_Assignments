GLA UNIVERSITY GREATER NOIDA


About

GLA University, Greater Noida is a higher-education campus located in Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. The university focuses on providing industry-oriented education, practical learning, innovation, and career development. Its academic approach emphasizes real-world applications, projects, internships, and interaction with industry professionals.

Programs

GLA University, Greater Noida offers programs in areas such as Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Electronics and Computer Engineering, BBA, MBA, and MCA. For students interested in technology, B.Tech programs include CSE and CSE with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, providing opportunities to develop programming, problem-solving, and industry-relevant technical skills.

Campus Life

Campus life at GLA University provides students with opportunities to participate in academics as well as extracurricular activities. Students can take part in cultural events, arts, music, drama, clubs, leadership activities, and various sports. Sports facilities and activities such as cricket, volleyball, athletics, chess, carrom, and table tennis contribute to an active and engaging student environment.

Admissions

Admission to GLA University, Greater Noida involves registration, payment of the registration fee, and the applicable selection process. For the 2026 batch, the university lists programs including B.Tech CSE, B.Tech CSE-AIML, B.Tech Electronics and Computer Engineering, AI-Enabled BBA, AI-Enabled MBA, and MCA-AIML. Depending on the program, applicants may need to appear for the GLA Entrance Test (GLAET) followed by an interview and document submission.
